Approach

Every assignment follows four steps, with validation points along the way.

  1. 01

    Scope

    Understand the question, review the documentation, set the indicators and the method.

  2. 02

    Collect

    Build the tools, train the teams, monitor data quality day by day.

  3. 03

    Analyse

    Clean, weight and analyse the data with reproducible, documented scripts.

  4. 04

    Report

    Clear report, key messages, dashboards and a validation workshop.
